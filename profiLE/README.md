# Clearfeed Labs
Reliable, compliant data feeds built on official and documented public sources. No logins, no proxies, no gray-area scraping.

## Our Apify Actors
- Grant & Fellowship Radar: Grants.gov, NSF and NIH opportunities with deadline alerts
- Internship & New Grad Jobs Feed: entry-level roles with H-1B sponsor signals
- ATS Jobs Feed: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Recruitee postings
- SEC Form 4 Insider Trades and SEC Form D Funding Feed
- Hail & Storm Leads: ZIP codes hit by hail, wind and tornadoes
- Local Business Leads and Company Contact Enrichment
