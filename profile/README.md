# Clearfeed Labs
Reliable data feeds built on official and documented public sources. No logins, no proxies, no gray-area scraping.

## Our Apify Actors
- [ATS Jobs Feed](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/ats-jobs-feed): Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Recruitee and more ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/ats-jobs-feed-examples))
- [Internship & New Grad Jobs Feed](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/entry-jobs-feed): entry-level roles with H-1B history ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/entry-jobs-feed-examples))
- [SEC Form D Funding Feed](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/form-d-funding-feed) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/form-d-funding-feed-examples), [Demo video](https://youtu.be/kO95b90vcAE)) and [SEC Form 4 Insider Trades](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/form4-insider-trades) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/form4-insider-trades-examples))
- [Company Contact Enrichment](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/company-contact-enrichment) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/company-contact-enrichment-examples)) and [Local Business Leads](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/local-business-leads) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/local-business-leads-examples))
- [Grant & Fellowship Radar](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/grant-fellowship-radar): Grants.gov, NSF and NIH ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/grant-fellowship-radar-examples))
- [Clinical Trial Tracker](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/clinical-trial-tracker): ClinicalTrials.gov changes ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/clinical-trial-tracker-examples))
- [Research Lab Finder](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/paper-to-lab-research-finder): funded labs by topic from NIH and NSF grants ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/paper-to-lab-research-finder-examples))
- [Money Trail](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/money-trail-federal-awards): federal contracts and grants from USAspending ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/money-trail-federal-awards-examples))
- [Political Ad Spend Tracker](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/political-ad-spend-tracker) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/political-ad-spend-tracker-examples)) and [House Voting Record Tracker](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/legislator-record-tracker) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/legislator-record-tracker-examples))
- [Congress Issue Activity Tracker](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/congress-issue-activity-tracker): bills each member sponsored on an issue and how far they got ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/congress-issue-activity-tracker-examples))
- [311 Complaints by District](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/district-pain-point-tracker) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/district-pain-point-tracker-examples))
- [Storm & Hail Leads by ZIP](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/storm-roofing-feed) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/storm-roofing-feed-examples))
- [Retirement Place Scorer](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/retirement-place-scorer) ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/retirement-place-scorer-examples))
- [Open Dataset Finder](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/open-dataset-finder): search Data.gov, Socrata portals and Hugging Face at once ([examples](https://github.com/clearfeedlabs/open-dataset-finder-examples))
- [Apify Store Opportunity Radar: Gaps, Prices & Alerts](https://apify.com/clearfeedlabs/apify-store-market-intel): finds Apify Store gaps, pricing and alerts from public Store API aggregate metrics and its own scores ([site](https://clearfeedlabs.com/actors/apify-store-market-intel/))
